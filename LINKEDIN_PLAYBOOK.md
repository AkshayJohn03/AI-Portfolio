# LinkedIn Playbook — Creative Technologist × Production AI

*The positioning: you are not "an AI engineer" (crowded) and not "a designer" (undervalued).
You are the rare bridge — **a Creative Technologist who ships production AI systems, measured
like an engineer and presented like a designer.** Everything below serves that one sentence.*

---

## Phase 1 — The 7-post series (6–8 weeks, one per week)

**Cadence:** Tue/Wed 9:00–10:30 IST. Format: document carousel (10–12 slides) or native video
(cut each 6-min brag episode to a 60–90s hook + link). One metric per post — never two stories.

| # | Hook | System | The number that carries it |
|---|------|--------|---------------------------|
| 1 | "40 hours in PowerPoint became 45 seconds. Here's the system." | BrandMorph | 43 tests, 65 audited changes |
| 2 | "Most RAG demos are wrappers. Mine is measured." | RAG_showcase | dense 0.41 → hybrid 0.94 recall |
| 3 | "Who grades the grader?" | VerdictAI | planted −19% caught, exit 1 |
| 4 | "Your AI platform needs a flight recorder." | ForensiQ | blame top-1 100% |
| 5 | "I attacked my own AI with 25 attacks." | RedForge | ASR 92% → 0% |
| 6 | "My AI bills can't surprise me anymore." | AegisGate | 484 rps, p95 60ms, measured |
| 7 | "The whole platform, in one scroll." | PipelineViz launch | the map itself |

Post rules: first line ≤ 8 words and contrarian or numeric · one idea per slide · last slide =
one link · 3 niche tags (#AIEngineering #CreativeTechnology #LLM) · reply to every comment in
the first 2 hours.

## Phase 2 — Website tie-in (your revamp)

Structure that converts the portfolio into proof:
1. **Home** — one screen, the positioning sentence, one interactive element (embed PipelineViz).
2. **Selected systems** — 4 case studies (AegisGate, VerdictAI, RedForge, BrandMorph), each:
   problem → system → *measured outcome* → link to repo + video.
3. **The platform** — iframe/embed PipelineViz directly; it does the explaining.
4. **Writing** — republish the deep-dives (SEO + permanence).
5. **Now page** — what you're building this month (signals "alive" to recruiters).

The site itself is Exhibit A for the claim: design craft + AI content in one artifact.

## Phase 3 — Free network & signal loops (ongoing)

- **Comment loop (10/week, 15 min/day):** substantive comments on AI-engineering and
  design-engineering posts (Latent Space circle, Eugene Yan, Simon Willison, swyx, design-
  engineering folks). Comments build reach faster than posts when you have <3k followers.
- **One deep-dive article** on your site/dev.to: *"Measured, not claimed: an eval gate for LLM
  releases"* — the VerdictAI story end-to-end. This becomes the evergreen link.
- **GitHub profile README** with the platform map + pinned repos (recruiters land there first).
- **Lightning talk** at a Bengaluru AI/dev meetup: *"The AI platform you can audit"* — 10 slides,
  all measured numbers. Talks convert to inbound better than any post.
- **Showcase submissions (free):** PipelineViz to GSAP showcase / Three.js journey galleries;
  the brag-video series to the latent-spaces (brag) community that built the tool.

## Phase 4 — Proof loop (monthly)
- One **ship-log post** per month: what shipped, numbers, one lesson.
- Watch: profile views, inbound quality (engineers/recruiters vs. course-sellers), saved-count
  on carousels. Adjust hooks, never the evidence discipline.

---

## What NOT to do
- No engagement-bait, no "AI will replace you" posts, no course funnels — they poison the
  measured-engineer brand.
- Don't post all 7 in two weeks; the series only works if the profile foundation ships first.
- Don't crosspost LinkedIn copy to X/Twitter unedited — different cadence, different culture.
