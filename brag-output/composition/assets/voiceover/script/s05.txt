Station two: VerdictAI. The critic who trains the taste-testers. Companies use AI to grade AI answers. But who grades the grader? VerdictAI grades against a rubric, a written marking scheme. It measures where the machine disagrees with humans, using Cohen's kappa. Agreement beyond luck. Zero is a coin flip; one is perfect. And it stands guard: if an update makes answers worse, a regression, the C I gate fires and blocks the release. The bad model never ships.
