Station three: ForensiQ. The C C T V and the health inspector. Every request leaves a trace: a tree of spans recording what was searched, what was found, what was generated. When answers go bad, ForensiQ reads the traces, names the failure from its taxonomy, a catalogue of named failure types, and points at the guilty stage. Rules classify the obvious; the AI judges only the ambiguous leftovers. On planted failures: precision and recall of one point zero. It found everything, and everything it found was real.
